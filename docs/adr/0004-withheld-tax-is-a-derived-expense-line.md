# Withheld tax is a derived expense line of its Transaction

Trade Republic gives interest and Saveback gross in `amount` and the tax it kept back in a separate `tax` column (interest 13.68 with tax -3.42). The owner wants the gross amount under the row's Category and the tax as an expense under Steuern → Kapitalertragsteuer, so income, spending and the Savings rate see the net effect. We keep one Transaction with the tax in `origin.taxCents` (already stored on import), and the spending queries (`counted` in `src/core/spending.ts`) derive a second, expense-only line from it at query time; the Umsätze list shows it under the amount as "Steuer". We chose this over storing a second Transaction per taxed row, which would break the one-row-one-Transaction duplicate key, give undo and manual Categories two things to keep in sync, and appear in the Uncategorized inbox and Transfer suggestions. The tax line follows its row: it counts whatever Category the owner gives the row and for Trades, never for Transfers, falls back to Nicht kategorisiert if Kapitalertragsteuer is deleted, and goes away with the import.
